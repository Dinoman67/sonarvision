Architecture · SonarVision System Overview

Side-Scan Sonar Marine Debris Intelligence

End-to-end edge & geospatial architecture for real-time acoustic shadow debris detection with YOLOv8-ESI attention and Leaflet GIS mapping.

CORE INNOVATION

Acoustic Shadow Attention

YOLOv8-ESI integrates Squeeze-and-Excitation attention into the C2f backbone, learning acoustic shadow geometry and backscatter intensity gradients rather than RGB brightness cues.

PROCESSING PIPELINE

Tiling & Geo-Referencing

  • FastAPI slides 256×256 inference windows over massive survey mosaics
  • Pixel coordinates map directly to WGS84 / UTM coordinates
  • Exports survey-grade PDF, GeoJSON, and CSV reports

EDGE DEPLOYMENT

Field-Ready Footprint

Quantized to 6.2 MB ONNX FP16 (3.3M parameters). Runs offline at 15–30 FPS on Raspberry Pi 3/4/5 hardware directly aboard marine survey vessels.
